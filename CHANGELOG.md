# Change Log

**1.4.0** _(01.10.2026)_
- Eyeballer: a tonal balance meter (Shadow / Mid / Light) above the Brightness and Light & Shadow views, with targets under Options > Balance target: 60/30/10, Low-key, High contrast, Spotlight, Moody even, Mid-key and High-key. The tip names the tone that is furthest off and what to change in the lighting.
- Eyeballer: finds the render inside the Redshift RenderView reliably, including dark-grey and black backgrounds.
- Eyeballer: says why when the engine fails to start (Help > Open engine log), remembers scope height and Adjustments, and keeps menus and floating windows over the viewport out of the analysis.

**1.3.0** _(27.09.2026)_
- Added Ease: a curve editor for keyframe easing. Bezier, Custom (multi-point), Bounce, Elastic and Steps tabs, a speed graph, live preview, Apply / Copy / Paste Reversed on the selected keys, and a preset library with profiles (import / export).

**1.2.0** _(27.09.2026)_
- Added Library: one browser for materials (built as OpenPBR), HDRIs, imperfections, light maps, gobos, bokeh, IES and LUTs. It replaces HDRI; the HDRI command still opens it.
- Eyeballer: views and scopes survive docking and tabbing.

**1.1.0** _(27.09.2026)_
- Added Eyeballer and Timelapse.
- Render Regions: Fit to Selection follows the camera's current position.

**1.0.0** _(27.09.2026)_
- First public release: Axis, Snap to Floor, Solo, Camera Toggle, Render Regions, Rename, Icon Color, Image As Plane, HDRI, PuzzleMatte, Localize Textures.
